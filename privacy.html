Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-26

This GST calculator (when hosted publicly) does not collect personal information by default. The site runs entirely client-side; calculations are performed in your browser and no inputs are sent to any server unless you enable analytics or other services.

Analytics

Ads

If ads (e.g., Google AdSense) are enabled, ad providers may collect data according to their own policies. Please see the ad provider's privacy policy for details.
